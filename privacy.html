Privacy Policy

Plex Mirror is a personal tool used only by its owner.

What it accesses

With your permission, it accesses the YouTube account you sign in with, only to create playlists and add videos to them.

How data is stored

Sign-in tokens and playlist information are stored only on the owner's own computer. Apart from the YouTube requests needed to update your playlists, nothing is sent to any other server.

Sharing

No data is shared, sold, or used for any other purpose.

Removing access

You can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
